成功标准Success

成功的意思是每个交付物在它那一周做出来、带着事先登记的数，不是「模型一定赢」。如果模型在组合里打不赢先来先做，这也是答案：系统就用先来先做，用你的眼睛和清单当最后一道门。Success means that each deliverable exists in its week, with its registered number. It does not mean that the model must win. If the model does not beat first come first served in the portfolio, that is also an answer. 术语Glossary

30 分钟 pivot30 minute pivot

30 分钟图上出现 3 根或更多连续阴线之后，第一根收在自己上三分之一的阳线（pivot 那根）。买入止损单挂在它的高点上方 1 个最小价位；止损放在这段形态的最低点下方 1 个最小价位；挂单有效到下一个交易日收盘，成交前跌破形态最低点就撤单。On the 30 minute chart, after 3 or more down bars in a row, the first up bar that closes in its top third (the pivot bar). Put a buy stop order 1 tick above its high. Put the stop 1 tick below the low of the pattern. The order stays until the close of the next session. If the price goes below the pattern low before the fill, cancel the order.

扫描器Scanner

你现在实盘用的程序。盘中每根 30 分钟 K 线收盘后，它列出符合严格 pivot 规则的股票。The program that you use live. After each 30 minute bar, it lists the stocks that meet the Strict Pivot rule.

严格 pivotStrict Pivot

扫描器现在认的 pivot：股票在扫描器的名单上（相对强度前 20%、趋势向上），前一天收盘离 21 日指数均线不太远，回调深度至少 0.4 个日 ATR，止损距离小于前 20 天平均日内振幅的 60%。The pivot that the scanner accepts now. The stock is on the scanner's list (relative strength in the top 20%, an up trend). The prior close is not too far above the 21 day exponential average. The pullback depth is at least 0.4 daily ATR. The stop distance is less than 60% of the average daily range of the last 20 days.

相对强度百分位Relative strength percentile

按前一天收盘，把一只股票过去 12 个月的加权涨幅（近几个月权重更大），在当天的流动股票池里排名。池子是美国上市的普通股和外国公司的存托凭证，股价 10 美元以上，50 天平均每天成交额 2,000 万美元以上。At the prior close, the rank of a stock's weighted 12 month return (recent months count more) in that day's liquid pool. The pool is US listed common stock and depositary receipts of foreign companies, with a price above $10 and an average daily dollar volume above $20 million over 50 days.

ATR14ATR14

最近 14 天的平均真实波幅，表示这只股票正常一天动多少。The average true range of the last 14 days. It shows the normal daily move of the stock.

RR

一笔交易的盈亏除以计划的风险（买价到止损的距离乘股数）。赚 3R 就是赚了计划风险的 3 倍。The profit or loss of a trade, divided by the planned risk (the distance from entry to stop, times the shares). A gain of 3R is three times the planned risk.

赢家Winner

pivot 当天和之后 60 个交易日里，最高收盘比 pivot 那根 K 线的收盘价高 N 个 ATR14。N 在第 2 周定，建议 10；按之前的回测，约 15% 的严格 pivot 是这样的赢家，每年都差不多。Within the pivot session and the next 60 sessions, the highest close is at least N ATR14 above the close of the pivot bar. Set N in week 2; 10 is recommended. In earlier backtests, about 15% of Strict Pivots are such winners, and the rate is almost the same each year.

变体Variation

生成形态的一条规则。严格 pivot 是第一个变体，当基准；其他例如回踩 10 日指数均线、回踩 50 日简单均线、VCP 的 cheat 买点。One rule that finds setups. The Strict Pivot is the first variation and the baseline. Other examples: a pullback to the 10 day exponential average, a pullback to the 50 day simple average, a VCP cheat entry.

VCP 和 cheatVCP and cheat

VCP 是 Minervini 的波动收缩形态：每次回调都比上一次浅。cheat 是形态完成之前、在最后一次收缩里提早买的点，按买点在形态里的高低分三种：low cheat 在形态下部，mid cheat 在中部，high cheat 在接近形态顶部、正式突破之前。VCP is Minervini's volatility contraction pattern: each pullback is smaller than the one before. A cheat is an early entry in the last contraction, before the pattern completes. The three kinds differ by their height in the pattern. A low cheat is in the lower part. A mid cheat is in the middle. A high cheat is near the top, before the real breakout.

Stage 2Stage 2

股票的上升阶段：价格在向上的长期均线之上。The advancing stage of a stock: the price is above a rising long term average.

打标Tag

你给每张图评 1 到 5 分、勾清单、写会不会买和止损放哪。You give each chart a score from 1 to 5, tick the checklist, and write if you would buy and where the stop goes.

熟不熟Familiarity

每张图都要填：不认识、好像见过、认识。认得的图可能靠记忆而不是眼光，所以单独统计。Fill it for each chart: unfamiliar, maybe seen, or recognized. You can know a recognized chart from memory, not from your eye, so we count those charts apart.

封存和揭示Seal and reveal

一批图打完后，把评分文件的 sha256 指纹记进带日期的记录（封存），之后任何改动都看得出来；封存之后才给你看每张图后来的走势（揭示）。After a batch, record the sha256 fingerprint of the ratings file in a dated note (seal). After that, any change shows. Only after the seal do you see what each chart did next (reveal).

sha256 指纹sha256 fingerprint

由文件内容算出的一串字符；文件改了一个字，指纹就不同。把指纹写进一份带日期、可信的记录（例如一次提交），以后再算一次对照，就能证明文件在那天之后没改过。A string calculated from the content of a file. If one character changes, the fingerprint changes. Write the fingerprint into a dated record that you can trust (for example, a commit). Later, calculate it again and compare. This proves that the file did not change after that date.

样本外Out of sample

定模型或规则时没用过的数据。只有在这种数据上的结果才算检验。Data that was not used to set a model or a rule. Only results on such data count as a test.

先来先做First come first served

每天按提示出现的先后买，直到当天的上限：每天最多 3 只新股票，总风险最多占账户的 3%。Buy the alerts in the order they come, up to the daily limit: 3 new stocks each day at most, and 3% of the account as total risk at most.

LR+LR+

模型挑出的形态在赢家里的比例，除以它在非赢家里的比例。大于 1 表示模型挑得比随便挑好。The share of model picks among winners, divided by their share among non winners. A value above 1 shows that the model picks better than chance.

加权 kappaWeighted kappa

两组评分一致到什么程度：0 是碰巧一致，1 是完全一致。How well two sets of scores agree: 0 is chance agreement, 1 is full agreement.

bootstrap 区间Bootstrap interval

把数据按股票或按月重新抽样很多次，看结果在多大范围里变动；这里用 90% 区间。Resample the data many times, by stock or by month, and see how far the result moves. This plan uses a 90% interval.

Holm 校正Holm correction

同时做几个检验时，限制碰巧过关（误报）的校正：所有检验加起来的误报率不超过事先定的数。A correction that limits false positives when you do several tests at the same time. The false positive rate of all the tests together stays at or below a number that you set first.

回撤Drawdown

账户从最高点往下跌了多少；最大回撤是这段时间里跌得最深的一次。How far the account falls from its highest point. The maximum drawdown is the deepest fall in the period.

最小价位和滑点Tick and slippage

最小价位是价格能变动的最小单位，美股一般是 0.01 美元。滑点是成交价比挂单价差出来的部分。A tick is the smallest step of a price, usually $0.01 for US stocks. Slippage is the difference between the order price and the fill price.

提交Commit

提交是把文件在那一刻的样子存成一份带日期的快照。之后拿文件和这份可信的快照对照，就能发现有没有改动。A commit saves a snapshot of the files with a date. Compare the files with this trusted dated snapshot to find changes.

逻辑回归、浅决策树、交叉验证Logistic regression, shallow decision tree, cross validation

前两种是简单、看得懂每个特征作用的模型。交叉验证是把数据分成几份，轮流用其中一份检验、其余的训练，看模型在没见过的部分上学得多像。The first two are simple models. You can see what each feature does in them. Cross validation splits the data into parts. Each part in turn tests the model, and the other parts train it. It shows how well the model does on data that it did not see.

现在的规则（基准）Current rules (the baseline)

买入：挂单在 pivot 高点上方 1 个最小价位。止损：形态最低点下方 1 个最小价位，买入后不动。仓位：回调深度至少 0.7 个日 ATR 时每笔风险占账户 0.2%，0.4 到 0.7 时 0.1%，不到 0.4 不做；总风险最多 3%；账户从最高点回撤 8% 后每笔风险减半，账户回到新高才恢复。出场：收盘高出 21 日指数均线 3 个 ATR，第二天开盘卖一半；剩下的，从第二个交易日起，收盘跌破出场线就在第二天开盘卖；出场线每天收盘按 QQQ 状态重新选，黄色用 10 日指数均线，绿、琥珀、红用 8 日指数均线；最多持有 60 个交易日。每天最多 3 只新股票。Entry: a buy stop 1 tick above the pivot high. Stop: 1 tick below the pattern low. Do not move the stop after the entry. Size: risk 0.2% of the account for each trade when the pullback depth is at least 0.7 daily ATR, 0.1% from 0.4 to 0.7, and no trade below 0.4. Total risk is 3% at most. After an 8% drawdown from the account high, cut the risk for each trade by half. Restore the full risk only at a new account high. Exit: when a close is 3 ATR above the 21 day exponential average, sell half at the next open. From the second session, sell the rest at the next open after a close below the exit line. Select the exit line again at each close by the QQQ state: the 10 day exponential average for yellow, the 8 day exponential average for green, amber or red. Hold for 60 sessions at most. 3 new stocks each day at most.

QQQ 状态QQQ state

扫描器每天收盘后给纳指 100 ETF（QQQ）定的四种趋势状态：绿、黄、琥珀、红。现在它只决定出场线（黄色用 10 日指数均线，其他三种用 8 日），不决定做不做。The trend state of the Nasdaq 100 fund (QQQ) that the scanner sets after each close: green, yellow, amber or red. Now it sets only the exit line (the 10 day exponential average for yellow, the 8 day for the other three). It does not decide if you trade.
