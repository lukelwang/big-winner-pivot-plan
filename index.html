Routines

Each session (decision log, 10 to 15 minutes, from week 2)

  • Before the open (06:00 to 06:25 PT): From the scanner's list of the day, mark up to 10 stocks that you want to own for this move. Rank them 1 to 10. Write one line of reason for each.
  • During the session: Write one row for each Strict Pivot alert. Record the score from 1 to 5, buy or not, and the decision (take, pass on the chart, no room, not seen). Record the reason (checklist items). If one bar gives several scores of 4 or 5, write the order in which you would take them. Score first. After the model is frozen, the page shows the model's score only after your score.
  • On time: Write the row before the first outcome of the order (a fill, a cancel below the pattern low before a fill, or the expiry). If you did not see the alert, write “not seen”. Do not add a score later. The page records the checklist and the system version at that time.
  • After the close: Do not look at a summary. Look only at your own trades.

Each week

  • 10 minutes on Sunday: Read the card for next week. Check that the events it waits for occurred. If not, change the weeks by the rules in “If you fall behind”. Each week's hours include these 10 minutes.
  • AI test cards: Write a claim from the posts as a card. Claude records the card's sha256 fingerprint and then runs it one time. The data that a card can use opens by stage (see the red lines).

Each month (first weekend, about 20 minutes)

  • Compliance: Check the share of alerts that have a score. The target is 80% or more. If it is lower for two consecutive months, keep only the score and the decision in the form. Also check the counts, and the R and dollars of your own trades.
  • Do not read: a grouped summary of the log. Calculate it only on the registered dates. In 2027 Apr, read only the stop trading criteria and compliance. The decision comes in 2028 Mar.
